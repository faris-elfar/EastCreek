What We're Exploring

More than a house.
A place that takes care of you.

Many people in New Jersey are living alone — far from family, with no one nearby to check in. East Creek Living is an early effort to change that, with simple, dignified housing and a few basic services built in.

A real place to live

Private or shared rooms in a residential home setting — not a clinical facility. Somewhere you can have your own space and still have people around you.

Halal meals envisioned

The proposed model includes meals prepared with halal ingredients. The goal is to avoid worrying about what's in your food or cook for yourself every day.

Weekly nurse visits proposed

The concept includes weekly wellness check-ins by a nurse, subject to final service arrangements. This is not a nursing home.

Community around you

Neighbors who share your values. Common spaces to sit, talk, and not be alone. Help with the small things that become hard to manage on your own.

"They need a place where they feel safe and belong — and where they find someone who preserves their dignity and accommodates their weakness."
— Mohammed Elbenni, Community Leader · North Brunswick, NJ
